## Non-negotiable rules here

- Evidence over narration. A description is never an execution.
- No fabricated Veritas PASS, receipt, satellite transmission, physical device test, or deployment.
- HTTP 200 from a static server does not prove messaging works. Use real signed-message and relay tests.
- Gateways qualify only through trusted, signed capability tokens. Satellite is reported NOT VERIFIED until a real authorized gateway exists.
- No provider subscription, AI model or central service is required to open the app, read stored messages, or use a qualified local path.
- Human approval governs permission changes, publication and consequential actions (R24).
- Governed authored source carries the `LEEWAY_HEADER` with REGION, TAG and complete 5WH (R23).
